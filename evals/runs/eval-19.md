# Eval 19 (run 2026-09-24): UK PhD, thesis by publication, three papers

Prompt: "Outline my PhD thesis for a UK university: how remote work affects mid-career promotion in Indonesian banks. I plan three papers."

---

One question: **which university?** UK regulations on theses by publication differ by institution. I have not waited for the answer; the full outline is below and every institution-specific rule is marked `[VERIFY]`.

**Form chosen: thesis by publication (integrated / article-based).** You plan three papers, so each paper becomes a chapter, framed by an integrating introduction and a general discussion. If your university only accepts a monograph, the same three studies become three analysis chapters with no other change to the logic.

**The UK bar:** a doctorate must show an original contribution to knowledge, of a quality that merits publication (QAA, *Framework for Higher Education Qualifications*, level 8 descriptor). The integrating chapters are where you prove the three papers add up to one contribution.
