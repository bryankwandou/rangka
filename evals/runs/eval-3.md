# Eval 3 — 60-second TikTok: outline before choosing a thesis title

Prompt: "Outline for a 60-second TikTok explaining why students should outline before choosing a thesis title."

Classification: en · Family E (timed media) · reader: final-year students scrolling TikTok · mode: from scratch · depth: Cepat (video under 3 min), plus E6 checks.

---

## 1. Core map

**After watching, the viewer can:** build a quick outline (gap → research questions → method) before proposing a thesis title.

| Node | Content |
|---|---|
| Should | Student proposes a title that already matches a clear problem, questions, and method |
| Is | Student picks a catchy title first, then struggles to fit a problem and method to it `[CHECK: common experience; do not present as a statistic]` |
| Gap | Title chosen before structure |
| Action | Save the video and try the 3-step outline on their own topic |

## 2. Outline

| Time | Section | Job | Visual | Audio/VO | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Hook | Stop the scroll | Student deleting a title in a document | "Your thesis title isn't rejected because of the words." | "Title rejected again?" |
| 3–10 s | Context | Why it matters | Crossed-out title, supervisor comment "too broad" (mock note, not a real document) | "It's rejected because nothing sits under it yet." | "No structure = weak title" |
| 10–25 s | Point 1: Gap | Value | Two boxes: "Should" vs "Is" | "First, write how things should be and how they actually are. The difference is your problem." | "1. Should vs Is = your problem" |
| 25–40 s | Point 2: Questions + method | Value | Arrow from problem to 2 questions, then to "survey / interview" | "Turn that problem into 1–3 questions, and pick a method that can answer them." | "2. Problem → questions → method" |
| 40–52 s | Point 3: Title last | Value | Title assembled from blocks: output + context + location | "Only now write the title, from what you'll actually study." | "3. Title comes LAST" |
| 52–60 s | Payoff + CTA | Close the hook's promise | Finished outline next to a clean title | "Structure first, and the title writes itself. Save this for your proposal." | "Save this for your proposal" |

Total: 3 + 7 + 15 + 15 + 12 + 8 = **60 s**.

Length hint: VO ≈ 110–130 words `[CHECK: read aloud with a timer]`.
